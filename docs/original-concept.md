

\# Project Strategy: BEACON

\*\*Target:\*\* Consumer Health Hackathon / YC S26 RFS  

\*\*Document Standard:\*\* ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English)



\---



\## 1. Product Vision



BEACON is a consumer application for personalized health data. 

It converts complex 50-page medical reports into two clean documents:

1\. \*\*Patient Dashboard:\*\* An interactive visual summary for the consumer.

2\. \*\*Physician Brief:\*\* A one-page clinical summary for the doctor.



The system requires zero prompts from the user.



\---



\## 2. The Core Problem



Consumers buy direct-to-consumer genetic tests and comprehensive blood panels. 

Companies send consumers large PDF reports. 



Patients face two issues:

\* \*\*Confusion:\*\* Patients cannot understand 50 pages of medical jargon.

\* \*\*Short Visits:\*\* Doctors have 8 minutes per appointment. They cannot read 50-page reports.



Result: Personalized health data is wasted. Patients get no actionable care plan.



\---



\## 3. Product Principles (What NOT to Build)



\* \*\*No Chatbots:\*\* Do not build a "Chat with PDF" tool. Patients do not know what questions to ask. Doctors will not read chat logs.

\* \*\*No Doctor Interruption:\*\* Do not build tools that fight or correct doctors during visits.

\* \*\*No Manual Prompting:\*\* The user uploads a file, and the application generates the outputs automatically.



\---



\## 4. Key User Artifacts



\### Artifact 1: Patient Dashboard (Consumer View)

\* Uses simple language and visual status indicators.

\* Groups health data into biological systems:

&#x20; \* Cardiovascular Risk

&#x20; \* Metabolic Health

&#x20; \* Genomic Variants

&#x20; \* Pharmacogenomics (Drug Response)

\* Shows clear, evidence-based lifestyle actions.



\### Artifact 2: Physician Brief (Doctor View)

\* High-density, one-page document.

\* Formatted like a standard medical record.

\* Displays:

&#x20; \* Out-of-range biomarkers with reference ranges.

&#x20; \* Pertinent genetic variants using medical notation.

&#x20; \* Clinical considerations for the doctor to review in 45 seconds.
